# Nordschleife Home banner outline

The owner requested a recognizable Nürburgring circuit outline on 9 October 2026. The earlier `circuit` Home banner revision used the Nordschleife geometry from [Nordschleife.svg on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nordschleife.svg), authored by Richard Walledge (Darkdoc). The author released this work into the public domain worldwide. The source page also grants unrestricted use where public-domain dedication is not legally recognized, subject to applicable law.

The original is retained byte-for-byte in `nordschleife-Darkdoc-source.svg`: 13182 bytes; SHA-1 `39530f66c0fcb12f7e829646af23a240e68daa63`, matching the upstream file record. Its source URL is `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8f/Nordschleife.svg`.

The runtime asset is `static/assets/images/template/home-section-nordschleife-outline-v2.svg`: 5074 bytes; SHA-256 `5738c3b8364d1e4dd9ea348c9145dc2edc63df1629b2edda38ce1c69115c1bdd`. It extracts `pathnord`, retains its coordinates, joins the small cartographic bridge gaps, closes the circuit and uses a white outline with one red accent. It omits labels, roads, grids and the other circuit layouts. The decorative presentation contains no logo, embedded heading or affiliation claim.

The earlier circuit revision used a generated asphalt background with one complete Nordschleife watermark on the right. The final owner-requested finish removes that map and pairs the admired left kerb with a matching photographic edge on the right; see [final polish](home-section-polish-2026-10-09.md). Both SVGs remain retained for history and reuse. Live heading/button typography and destinations remain native page content. The generated asphalt plate has no embedded map; its source and encoding are recorded in [the Home banner generation history](home-section-comparison-2026-10-08.md).

The source dedication applies to the upstream circuit geometry. This is a decorative map illustration, not an operator-approved racing or navigation plan. Reuse the retained source record when personalizing client templates.
