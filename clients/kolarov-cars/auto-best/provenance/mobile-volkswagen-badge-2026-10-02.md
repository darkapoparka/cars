# Mobile Volkswagen badge

`static/assets/images/brand-curated/volkswagen-badge-cardog.svg` is an unchanged
copy of Cardog's optimized `Volkswagen Icon.svg` at revision
`05c4001e83a09a189721a0851546291f71f7e31c`.

[Pinned source](https://github.com/cardog-ai/icons/blob/05c4001e83a09a189721a0851546291f71f7e31c/core/optimized/Volkswagen%20Icon.svg)
uses the retained [Cardog MIT notice](cardog-icons-LICENSE.txt).
The mark identifies Volkswagen and remains its owner's trademark; this source
is not a manufacturer download or generated identity.

The transparent SVG is 512 by 512px, with measured visible bounds
`[79, 78, 434, 433]`. Its SHA-256 is
`c80577703c4ab508b561edb653a3e2a3226bd22bfee1345a3886a065c76f10d1`.
Geometry and source colors are preserved. The existing visible-bounds frame
gives it the same 46px optical size as the current Mercedes-Benz and BMW marks.

`homeBrandCards` and `mobileFeaturedBrandLabels` in `src/lib/data/home.ts`
provide the mobile Home trio: Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Volkswagen.
`BrandSection.svelte` keeps Volkswagen below 768px and retains the desktop
catalog, existing Mercedes-Benz/BMW assets, native make links and All tile.
Stock counts continue to come from inventory; no Volkswagen vehicle is invented.
Audi remains available in inventory and the desktop catalog.
