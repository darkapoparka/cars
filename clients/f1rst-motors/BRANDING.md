# F1rst Motors — internal proposal branding

The live F1rst Motors identity was visually inspected, but the dealer's published terms state that site logos/images/content require prior written consent. For this unpublished owner-review concept, the committed `dealer/logo-*.svg` files are therefore a distinct professional proposal family, not copied official artwork and not presented as an approved rebrand.

The black/ivory/gold family uses a geometric F1 monogram and consistent light/dark treatments. `dealer/stock/*.svg` are clearly labelled concept-preview illustrations tied to dated public listing facts; they are not dealer photographs or another dealer's stock.
