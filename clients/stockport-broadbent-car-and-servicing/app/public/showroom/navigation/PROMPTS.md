## Finance

Saved original: `finance-v4.png`; runtime asset: `finance-v4.webp`. An initial draft was refined after inspection at navigation-tile size; its original remains in the generated-image archive and the task runtime folder.

Create a premium photorealistic automotive cutout for a small Finance navigation tile in a charcoal-and-white single-dealer mobile app. Main subject: a clean unbranded pearl-white sedan in front-right three-quarter view, slightly elevated camera, facing right, with a small graphite car key fob and one neat plain document in the foreground to suggest financing. Match the lighting and realism of automotive studio product photography: restrained neutral whites, silver, black, soft highlight reflections, realistic wheel and body geometry. Wide compact composition approximately 3:2, car occupying 80% of the canvas width, complete silhouette and wheels visible. Key and document remain secondary but legible at 60px display size. Tight framing with a little transparent breathing room. True transparent background, no studio room, no plinth, no scenery, no people, no calculator, no coins, no banknotes. No logos, no text, no numbers, no labels. This is illustrative navigation art, not a photograph of stock. Create one image only.
