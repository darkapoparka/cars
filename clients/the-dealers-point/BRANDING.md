# The Dealers Point — demo branding

Published identity was visually inspected on the dealer's site. The committed `dealer/logo-*.svg` files are a professional proposal refresh for this unpublished concept, not an assertion that they are official original artwork. The geometric TDP monogram, dark/light treatments and red accent are kept consistent across all three app copies.

The ten `dealer/stock/*.svg` files are generated concept-preview illustrations labelled as such. They are not photographs of the advertised cars and do not borrow another dealer's inventory imagery. Listing titles, prices and kilometres come from the dated public TDP stock page and must be reconfirmed.
