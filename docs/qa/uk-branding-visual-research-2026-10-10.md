## Media and stock handling

Public visibility does not establish permission to copy dealership logos or vehicle photographs. All photo/media licences remain unknown in this note. No inspected photo is claimed to be owner-supplied or licensed.

Public stock facts may be recorded with their source and observation date, GBP prices, mileage units, and an availability caveat. If no permitted photograph exists, an explicitly labelled locally generated body-style illustration can accompany those facts. It must say that it is an illustration, not a photograph of the advertised vehicle; it must not imply verified exact condition, equipment, colour or registration.

Keep any proposed identity separate from claims of business approval. Logo acceptance, asset permission, public contact verification, build success, live hosting, and visual acceptance are separate facts.
