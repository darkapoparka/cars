## Presentation and UK build handoff

The requested six styles are **Auto Best, Modern, Import, App, Mobile and Signature (karento-best)**. The entry recommendation only chooses which to show first. The [sister's sales guide](../docs/uk-sales-board-2026-10-10.md) covers each style, a 90-second demonstration, discovery questions, objections and what a YES needs next. Its companion interactive board is saved separately as the presentation artifact.

Confirm four things with a dealer before adaptation: whether it owns any website/domain; who can approve branding and stock use; how stock is maintained; and where actual enquiries must arrive. Demonstrate stock, one vehicle detail and the viewing action. A design demo does not establish a live stock feed, delivered enquiry, finance approval or private CRM integration.

UK configuration should use the dealer's **GB location, en-GB, actual GBP prices, verified mileage units, UK postcodes and +44 contacts**. Language choice should remain explicit and persistent. Visitor location can help suggest language; it must not change the dealer's business country, actual prices or original distance data. The guide records the proposed behaviour; implementation remains later work.

Builds wait for accepted immutable releases and a qualified **Cloudflare** publisher/pilot. This research changes no template lock, manifest, hosting, DNS or billing. Research flags remain **researchOnly=true, buildApproved=false, outreachApproved=false, contactHistory=not_checked**.
