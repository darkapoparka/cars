Record count, inventory evidence and qualified sales opportunities are three different measures. Nothing in this document guarantees sales, budget, business reputation or website conversion performance.

## Qualification before building

Use the following as an initial campaign screen, not an externally validated prediction model:

1. **Real, distinct account.** Match trading name, country/city, official domain, marketplace identity and public business contact route. Check existing Cars folders and the approved private CRM/history. Shared addresses or similar names alone do not establish a match. One business with several marketplace profiles should not receive duplicate speculative projects or messages.
2. **Relevant, active stock.** Prefer at least 30 unique available passenger cars/light trucks. This is an adjustable work-priority threshold, not a rule about business quality. Exclude sold, reserved, parts, duplicate and unsuitable categories. A premium specialist may be an explicitly approved exception. A marketplace ad count or homepage marketing statement is only a screening signal.
3. **A useful website opportunity.** Confirm no matched independent site, or demonstrate a specific current problem in mobile stock discovery, vehicle information, enquiry context, location clarity or content consistency. A dated appearance or use of a common template vendor is not sufficient on its own. State the observed issue separately from the proposed improvement.
4. **Fits the existing offer.** Check that Auto Best, Modern and Carwow can provide three meaningful concepts with branding/content changes and normal localization, not a concealed custom-platform rebuild. Identify required inventory-feed, dealer-management, finance, language and hosting needs before promising production capability.
5. **Safe to proceed.** Confirm authorized use of template/media assets, permitted source access, an appropriate business channel, absence of applicable suppression, and a separately approved build/send scope. Unknown private contact history is a blocker to assuming the dealer is new or safe to recontact, not evidence that no contact occurred.

Only promote to an approved build queue after these checks are documented in the appropriate private operations record. The public JSON entries currently remain research-only. Do not fabricate qualification, buying intent or website defects to make a region's list look more complete.

## Website audit evidence

For an existing website, inspect its home, inventory, a real vehicle detail, about/location and contact routes at 390 and 1440 px. Exercise search/filter, menu, image gallery, back navigation and enquiry entry without submitting to an external service. Record exact URL, timestamp, viewport, visible behavior and evidence location. Distinguish a reproducible failure from preference.

Check that any extracted issue is actually visible: repeated markup can represent hidden desktop/mobile variants; generic error text can belong to a hidden dialog. A blocked research browser, extraction failure, robots restriction or CAPTCHA is not proof that the dealer's customers see a broken page. Leave the visual result unverified when access is unavailable.

Useful findings include unavailable vehicles represented as in stock, wrong or inconsistent contact destinations, an enquiry that loses vehicle context, unusable filtering, missing location context, inaccessible overlays or unnecessary barriers to viewing a car. These are questions to test, not default accusations against the records in this batch.

Capture only the material necessary for the finding. Store private/contact-bearing evidence in approved private storage. Do not automatically mirror whole sites, publish private browser state or use screenshots as a license to reuse assets.

## Repeatable city/market research pass

Define the city/region, dealer type, inventory-size screen and source set before searching. Use permitted public business directories, marketplace dealer profiles and official sites; read platform terms and use an authorized API/feed where required. Never bypass access controls, CAPTCHA or rate limits.

For each candidate, record the source URL, retrieval date, source freshness and count meaning. Search exact business names with city and alternate spelling, inspect official social/profile website destinations, and check domain/business identity before declaring that no independent site was matched. Keep personal details out of the public research index.

Deduplicate against the four JSON files, existing client metadata and the designated private history. IDs are permanent account research identifiers; do not rename them whenever a slug or website changes. Preserve aliases and conflicting observations until a human-confirmed merge can be recorded. Similar domains in different countries must not be merged automatically.

A completed city pass means its declared source/query scope was reviewed and candidates dispositioned, not that the entire real-world market has been exhausted. Record source limits and unreviewed pages. Track researched, duplicate, unqualified, audit-needed and qualified counts privately; only aggregate or public-safe research dispositions belong here.

## Freshness and uncertain candidates

Proposed operating policy: refresh stock/domain evidence before each build, aiming for evidence no older than seven days where feasible; always recheck the exact recipient, channel and preview immediately before outreach. Older evidence can remain as history but must not be labeled current. Live feeds require separately implemented, authorized integrations.

The batch deliberately retains four reserves: Al Hamoor Al Thahabi (25 retrieved ads and location uncertainty), Car Finder 360 (24 ads and an existing site), Midlands Trade Centre (26 matches), and Keen Auto Mall (stale/conflicting source snapshots). These are not automatic build targets.

Other researched low-volume or unresolved examples are not counted among the forty: Used Autos of Orlando, SA Auto Sale and Mainland Auto Sales showed lower retrieved inventory totals; Machado Auto Sales had an independently identifiable site but no reliable own-stock total established in the bounded pass. Recheck before treating those old observations as an exclusion today. These are research dispositions, not judgements about reputation or sales interest.

## JSON contract and updating records

The four regional JSON files are the machine-readable public research source. `schemaVersion` is currently 1. Each file has `market`, `updatedAt`, `defaults`, `notes` and `leads`. Bulgaria also supplies a `legacyEvidence` URL.

Each record has a stable `id`, business `name`, `country`, nullable `city`, `priority`, `inventory`, `website`, `businessContactSource`, `evidenceNote`, `opportunityHypothesis`, nullable `existingClientPath`, `recommendedEntryTemplate` and `nextAction`. Optional fields include `region`, `userNominated`, `evidenceUrls` and inventory caveats/derivations. The file defaults supply all three template choices and research/approval limits; consumer code must apply them explicitly.

`inventory.count` is meaningful only alongside `kind`, its source URL, time fields, caveats and optional `comparison`. `dealer_stated_stock` is not an audited count. `derived_homepage_make_total` needs its derivation. `retrievedAt` is a research retrieval date, not necessarily the crawler date or proof of a live origin response. Historical `observedAt` values are preserved without being refreshed by a documentation edit.

`website.confidence` describes the stated observation; `high_for_presence_only` does not rate website quality. A null `website.url` means no matched domain in the described pass. A null `existingClientPath` does not rule out earlier projects elsewhere. A null city is unresolved, not a request to guess.

On updates, validate JSON syntax, unique IDs across all four files, country/file consistency, valid HTTP(S) source URLs, allowed priority values and recognized template keys. Keep the readable README counts/tables consistent in the same change. Use one coordinator for shared research-index edits; workers should return evidence instead of concurrently overwriting the same records.

This document specifies a manual workflow and data contract. It does not add schema enforcement, background research, scraping, CRM synchronization or a new CLI. Implementing any of those is a separate scoped task.
