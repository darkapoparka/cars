- `sourceLogoUrl`: none selected. Cazoo's generic fallback is rejected.
- `workingLogoPath`: [assets/broadbent-proposal-logo.png](assets/broadbent-proposal-logo.png).

- `approvalStatus`: generated independent proposal concept; no dealership approval or official logo status.
- `sourceMediaPermission`: not established. Public visibility and owner-photo attribution do not grant reproduction rights.

Preserve the exact name **Broadbent Car and Servicing**. The restrained charcoal/red concept references the observed premises visually; those colours are a proposal choice, not a verified historical brand palette. Root handles raster integration, actual small-size header inspection and favicon/touch-icon exports. Do not substitute another Broadbent garage's identity, a manufacturer's badge, a platform icon or a CSS/text-only logo.
