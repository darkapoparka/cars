# Mobile BMW roundel

`static/assets/images/brand-curated/bmw-roundel-cardog-mobile.svg` is an unchanged
copy of Cardog's optimized `BMW Logo.svg` at revision
`05c4001e83a09a189721a0851546291f71f7e31c`.

[Pinned source](https://github.com/cardog-ai/icons/blob/05c4001e83a09a189721a0851546291f71f7e31c/core/optimized/BMW%20Logo.svg)
uses the retained [Cardog MIT notice](cardog-icons-LICENSE.txt).
The supplied black ring, lettering, blue and white quarters are preserved.
The mark identifies BMW and remains its owner's trademark; this is a curated
icon source, not a manufacturer download or generated identity.

The transparent SVG is 512 by 512px, with visible bounds
`[78, 78, 434, 434]`. Its SHA-256 is
`5f96f5555de54a99855533608446bac06fb5f3af6155510f0e41e42c901d3291`.
The source geometry and colors are unchanged. It replaces the faint, low-resolution
bitmap only below 768px, using the existing 46px optical frame. Tablet and desktop
retain `assets/images/partner/parner12.png`.

`mobileBrandArtwork` in `src/lib/data/home.ts` owns the responsive source and
measured bounds. `mobileBrandLabels` shortens the Mercedes-Benz mobile caption
to Mercedes. `BrandSection.svelte` renders the compact caption only at phone
widths, while stock counts and links keep the canonical Mercedes-Benz make.
The mobile trio and native All inventory link are unchanged.
