The four-card layout retains the Cars collection artwork. Leasing uses App's `public/showroom/black/home-finance-v1.png`, encoded at 960px as WebP quality 85. Runtime crop tuples frame the handover and car/coins subjects. App provenance is recorded in `public/showroom/black/ASSETS.json`.

Import uses the existing Auto Best `menu-import-v2.webp` car/container composition. Both are selected independently through `leadSite.artwork.homeActionScenes`; the service-page heroes, call banners and desktop composition keep their own artwork selections. No new artwork was generated for this change.
