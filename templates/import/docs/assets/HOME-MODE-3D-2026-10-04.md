# Home mode 3D artwork — 4 October 2026

The owner explicitly requested generated 3D assets instead of the Home tab line icons. Four transparent cutouts use red lacquer, brushed silver and graphite details: a generic car for Buy, percent sculpture for Leasing, blank sales tag for Sell and globe with orbit arrow for Import. They are decorative interface artwork, not photographs of stock or claims about a dealer's inventory.

Generation used the built-in imagegen tool, with one call per asset and transparent_background enabled. The tool does not expose a model selector; no claim about the requested 2.5 model is made. [The final prompt set and asset metadata](HOME-MODE-3D-2026-10-04.prompts.json) records the exact prompts, generated source paths, original/output hashes and dimensions.

The original 1280px square PNGs remain at their recorded Codex generated_images paths and at L:/CODEX/cars/templates/import/runtime/desktop-home-3d-tabs-2026-10-04/originals/. Delivery files are 192px square WebP images. Existing Sharp resamples and encodes them at quality 90 / alphaQuality 100; it does not alter the generated objects, composition or transparent alpha. All four files together total 43676 bytes. The served assets live in the workspace.
